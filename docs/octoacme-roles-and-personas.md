# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery Lead / Scrum Master

### Role Summary
The Delivery Lead or Scrum Master facilitates agile ceremonies, removes impediments, and supports the team in maintaining a healthy delivery rhythm. They protect the team's focus and help optimize the flow of work from backlog to production.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and remove blockers that impede team progress
- Coach the team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Track velocity and help the team maintain sustainable pace
- Escalate risks and dependencies to the Project Manager when needed

### Goals
- Enable predictable, high-quality delivery each sprint
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement
- Reduce waste and unplanned interruptions

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Impediment logs and retrospective action items
- Coordination with Project Managers and Technical Lead on risks and capacity

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Works closely with **Project Managers** to align on timelines, risks, and dependencies
- Partners with **Developers** to remove blockers and uphold engineering practices
- Coordinates with **Product Managers** to ensure backlog readiness and sprint goal clarity

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead or Architect provides technical direction, ensures solution quality, and guides the team in making sound architectural decisions. They bridge the gap between product requirements and engineering implementation.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate the technical vision and architecture
- Review solution designs and significant code changes
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Ensure non-functional requirements (performance, security, scalability) are addressed
- Mentor developers and support knowledge sharing within the team

### Goals
- Deliver a maintainable, scalable, and secure technical solution
- Reduce rework caused by poor design decisions
- Align technical approach with business and product goals

### Typical Communication
- Architecture decision records (ADRs) and technical design docs
- Code and design review feedback
- Cross-team alignment sessions on shared services or infrastructure

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Collaborates with **Developers** on design, implementation guidance, and code reviews
- Advises **Product Managers** on technical feasibility and trade-offs
- Informs **Project Managers** of technical risks and dependencies that affect timelines

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
The Business Analyst bridges stakeholder needs and delivery work by gathering, documenting, and clarifying requirements. They ensure the team understands the problem space before building solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business requirements and user stories
- Facilitate requirement workshops and stakeholder interviews
- Clarify acceptance criteria and resolve ambiguities with stakeholders
- Maintain traceability between business needs and delivered features
- Support testing by validating that delivered work meets business requirements

### Goals
- Ensure the team builds the right thing for the right reasons
- Reduce rework caused by unclear or incomplete requirements
- Improve stakeholder confidence in delivery outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Requirement documents, user stories, and acceptance criteria
- Stakeholder interviews and review sessions
- Collaboration with Product Managers and Developers during backlog refinement

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Works with **Product Managers** to translate vision into detailed, actionable requirements
- Supports **Developers** by answering questions and clarifying edge cases during development
- Aligns with **Project Managers** to ensure scope changes are tracked and communicated

---

## UX / Design Lead

### Role Summary
The UX or Design Lead ensures that products are usable, accessible, and aligned with customer experience goals. They bring a user-centered perspective to the team and translate research insights into design solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity design assets
- Define and maintain design standards and component libraries
- Review implemented features for design and accessibility compliance
- Advocate for the end user throughout the development process

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible experiences that meet user needs
- Reduce usability issues discovered after release
- Maintain visual and interaction consistency across the product

### Typical Communication
- Design files, prototypes, and annotated specs shared with Developers
- User research summaries and usability test reports
- Design reviews with Product Managers and Business Analysts

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Collaborates closely with **Product Managers** to ensure designs reflect product strategy and user needs
- Works with **Developers** to ensure designs are implemented accurately and accessibly
- Partners with **Business Analysts** to incorporate user research into requirement refinement

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
The Release Manager coordinates release readiness, deployment activities, and stakeholder communication to ensure smooth and predictable software releases.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain the release calendar and coordinate release windows with stakeholders
- Confirm release readiness through sign-off checklists and go/no-go decisions
- Coordinate deployment activities across engineering, operations, and support teams
- Communicate release status, changes, and risks to stakeholders
- Manage rollback plans and post-release monitoring coordination

### Goals
- Deliver releases on schedule with minimal risk and disruption
- Improve release confidence through consistent process and clear communication
- Reduce the frequency and impact of post-release incidents

### Typical Communication
- Release notes and deployment runbooks
- Go/no-go meeting facilitation with leads and stakeholders
- Post-release status reports and incident retrospectives

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Coordinates with **Project Managers** to align release milestones with project timelines
- Works with the **Technical Lead** to assess deployment readiness and risk
- Communicates with **Product Managers** and **Stakeholders** on release scope and scheduling
